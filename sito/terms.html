Terms and support

UK House by House · an Instagram game with a 3D map · last updated: 21 November 2026

Customer support

Questions about a purchase, a refund or your house? Email privacy@ukhousebyhouse.com or send a direct message on Instagram to @ukhousebyhouse. Please include your Instagram username. We reply within 2 working days.

The game

UK House by House is a free game. If you comment your town and country on a post by @ukhousebyhouse on Instagram, you get a house on the map at ukhousebyhouse.com with your username. The full rules and how we use your data are in our rules and privacy notice. By playing or buying, you agree to these terms.

What you can buy

  • Since 21 November 2026 everything in the game shop is bought with credits (see credits rules) and is yours forever: "Your home" (decorate your house inside and out, with furniture), a villa or a castle on the map, vehicles for walking around town, clothes and accessories for your character, and furniture packs.
  • House decorations and rare houses (team colours, golden house, neon and other effects, or a one-of-a-kind design like a lighthouse, castle or windmill) are free on your own house once you've got "Your home", and you can change them whenever you like. As a gift for another player's house they cost 490 credits if you've got "Your home", or 990 credits if you haven't. Gifts show "🎁 from @your_account" above the other player's house, so both accounts must already have a house on the map. A house that already belongs to its owner's "Your home" can't receive gifts: they decorate it themselves.
  • Each rare house can be held by only one house per town. Each castle can be held by only one player per county: first come, first served.
  • Decorations, villas, castles and everything else in the shop are looks only: they don't give points and never count in elections (Prime Minister, First Minister, mayors and other roles). Nobody can buy roles.
  • Decorations bought in pounds before 21 November 2026 stay on the map until the end of their 30 days, with the +50 points that were promised (which never counted in elections either).
  • You can always try decorations for free on the map before buying.

Credits rules 💰

In short: credits are the game's currency. You get some free every day you visit, and you can buy more. They're only for items and features in the game: you can't buy points, votes, roles or places in the league table.

  • Credits (💰) are a virtual currency in the game: 100 credits are worth £1 in shop prices. They aren't money: you can't swap them for pounds, withdraw them, sell them or move them to another account. They stay in your account while the game is running.
  • Free credits: every day you sign in you get 2 credits. On the 7th day in a row you get 20 extra, and on the 30th 100 extra (and so on every 7 and every 30 days). Your account must be linked to an Instagram account with a house on the map. One reward a day per @, even if you use more than one account. The day changes at midnight UK time; if you miss a day, your streak starts again from 1.
  • Treasure hunt: when you walk around a town you can find treasure chests with a few free credits (up to 5 a day).
  • Top-ups are paid in pounds through Stripe (VAT included); the exact price is always shown before you pay. Credits arrive in your account straight away. You can top up a maximum of £150 every 30 days per account. If you're under 18, ask a parent or carer before topping up.
  • Refunds: credits are digital content delivered straight away. When you pay, we ask you to agree to immediate delivery and to confirm that you lose your 14-day right to cancel once you receive the credits. If you still get a refund or a chargeback, the credits from that top-up are taken off your balance: if you'd already spent them, your balance goes negative and the items you bought stay locked until it's back above zero. For any problem with a payment, please email privacy@ukhousebyhouse.com first: we'll sort it out. This doesn't affect your statutory rights as a consumer.
  • Fair use: one account per person. Creating fake accounts or using tricks to get more free credits means your free credits are reset and, in serious cases, your account is closed. Credits you bought are never taken away for this reason, unless the payment was fraudulent.
  • How we handle your email, account and credit transactions: see the privacy notice.

Games

"Guess the Town" and "Big Ben" are free daily games in the 🎮 Games tab. Your first game of the day counts: a win gives your house +5 points, and games count for at most 15 points a day in elections. The top 3 on each week's games leaderboard get +50/+30/+15 points for their house (not counted in elections) and 1st becomes Games Champion for a week. To get points you link your phone by commenting a code (like "HOUSE-1234") under one of our posts. One game a day per phone; tricks or fake accounts may be disqualified.

Shops and billboards for businesses

  • A local business can put a 3D shop or a billboard on the map of a town that already has houses, with its name, colour, logo, a short description and a WhatsApp button. The price depends on the size of the town and is shown before you pay (VAT included). It's a one-off payment for 30 days from approval: nothing renews by itself.
  • Each town has a limited number of shops and billboards. Your space is held once you've paid; we check every business before it goes live, normally within 24 hours. A shop takes a plot in town and keeps it; a billboard goes in the next free billboard spot in town (the best spots go first).
  • We don't accept betting, piracy, adult content, politics, weapons, prescription drugs, crypto or investments, loans, or anything offensive or misleading. If we don't approve your business, we refund you in full. You can also ask for a full refund within 7 days of payment if it hasn't gone live yet.
  • If you name your player account when you order, that house gets the points and Patron badge shown on the order page: they grow the house but don't count in elections.

Payment

Payments are processed securely by our payment provider (Stripe). We never see or store your card details. You'll get a receipt by email. If you're under 18, a parent or carer must make the purchase.

Cancellation and refunds

For digital content you normally have 14 days to cancel. When you ask for the decoration to start straight after payment, you agree that you lose that right once it starts. Even so, we'll refund you if you ask within 7 days of payment: just email privacy@ukhousebyhouse.com with your Instagram username, and the decoration is switched off. If a decoration doesn't appear on the map within 24 hours of payment, or the map is unavailable for most of its 30 days, we'll fix it or refund you in full. This doesn't affect your statutory rights as a consumer.

Gifts and fair use

Anyone who receives a gift can ask us to remove it at any time (the buyer is then refunded). We may remove a decoration, without refund, if it's used to harass another player, or if the house is removed from the map at its owner's request or for breaking the game rules.

Challenges and prizes

Sometimes we run short challenges, announced on @ukhousebyhouse with a countdown on the map (for example "Bring Your Mates": whoever brings the most new players before the deadline wins a decorated house). Unless the post says otherwise:

  • A new player counts for you if you tagged them in a comment on one of our posts and they get their first house on the map (by commenting their town and country) during the challenge, UK time. Each new player counts once, for the first person who tagged them.
  • The winner is whoever has the most new players when the challenge ends. On a tie, whoever reached that number first wins. Results come from the same map update that everyone can see.
  • Free to enter, no purchase needed. Fake, duplicate or bought accounts don't count, and we may disqualify anyone who tries to game the challenge.
  • The prize is a decoration of the winner's choice from the shop (including a rare house, if it's free in their town) for 10 days. It has no cash value and can't be exchanged. We contact the winner on Instagram; if they don't reply within 7 days, the prize goes to the runner-up.
  • Challenges are run by UK House by House only. They aren't sponsored, endorsed or run by Instagram or Meta.

Availability and changes

We do our best to keep the map online and up to date, but it may sometimes be slow or offline for maintenance. We may update the game, the prices of future purchases and these terms; purchases already made keep the terms that applied when you paid. If we ever close the game, we'll refund the days left on any active decoration bought in pounds, and we'll give at least 30 days' notice so you can use your credits.

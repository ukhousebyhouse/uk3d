Rules and privacy

UK House by House · an Instagram game with a 3D map · last updated: 27 October 2026

How the game works

If you comment your town and country on a post by the Instagram account @ukhousebyhouse, you get a house on the map at ukhousebyhouse.com with your username (@). You earn points by commenting, tagging mates, getting votes and posting stories that tag the account. Every week, whoever scores most gets the game's roles. Nobody can buy points or roles. Only places in the United Kingdom (England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland) can be on the map.

Non-partisan game: the roles (Prime Minister, First Minister, Mayor and so on) are made up and have no link to any real institution, political party or person.

Who we are and how to contact us

The data controller is the operator of the UK House by House project (Instagram account @ukhousebyhouse, website ukhousebyhouse.com).
For any question or request about privacy: privacy@ukhousebyhouse.com

What data we use

  • Your public Instagram username, if you comment on our posts or tag us in a story.
  • The public text of your comment, used only to work out your town and country, the mates you tagged and any votes.
  • Date and time of comments and story mentions, to work out the week's points.
  • For story mentions, Instagram sends us an account identifier: we don't save the story images.

We don't collect emails, phone numbers, location or any other private data through the game. The map only shows your username, the town you chose and your points.

Why we use it (purpose and lawful basis)

We only use this data to run the game: showing houses on the map and working out points, league tables and weekly roles. Taking part is voluntary: commenting your town and country is how you ask to join. Our lawful basis is legitimate interests in running the game that players ask to take part in (Article 6(1)(f) UK GDPR). We don't sell or share the data with anyone and we don't use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

Your data stays on the map while the game is running, or until you ask us to remove it. If a comment is deleted on Instagram, it stops counting at the next update.

Removing your house (deleting your data)

You can ask us to remove your house and all your data from the game at any time:

  • email privacy@ukhousebyhouse.com with your Instagram username, or
  • send a direct message on Instagram to @ukhousebyhouse.

We remove it within 7 days, at the next map update, and your account is left out of all future calculations too.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict and object to the use of your data. To use any of these rights, email privacy@ukhousebyhouse.com. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

The website

The site doesn't use tracking cookies or advertising tools. Your browser may save the house you marked as "this is my house" on your own device, just for convenience: you can clear it at any time in your browser settings. The site is hosted by Hostinger.

Anonymous statistics and errors

To know how many people visit the site we count visits anonymously, without cookies: we only save daily totals (number of visits, time, page, whether you came from the Instagram app or another site, and device type). To count unique visitors per day we use a code worked out from your IP address and browser together with a key that changes every day and is deleted the next day: we don't save your IP address and you can't be recognised on later days. If the map fails to load, the site sends us the error message and browser type so we can fix the problem.

Businesses on the map (shops and billboards)

If you ask to put your business on the map, we use the details you type into the form: the business name, type and town, colour, logo, description or message, and the business's WhatsApp number and Instagram (which are shown on the map), plus your email (which isn't shown and is only used to reply to you and for the payment). Payments are handled by Hotmart, which processes payment data as an independent controller: we only receive confirmation of the payment. We keep these details while the business is on the map and for as long as tax law requires; you can ask us to delete them at privacy@ukhousebyhouse.com.

Decorated houses and gifts

If you decorate a house or send a gift, we save the Instagram account of the house, your account (only for gifts: it appears on the map as "🎁 from @…") and the effects you chose. We only use accounts that already have a house on the map. Payments are handled by Hotmart, which processes payment data (including your email) as an independent controller: we only receive confirmation of the payment. The decoration stays visible for 10 days from payment; it's for looks only and gives no points or advantage in the game. Anyone who receives a gift can ask us to remove it with a message to @ukhousebyhouse or to privacy@ukhousebyhouse.com.

Your right to cancel: for digital content you normally have 14 days to cancel. When you ask for the decoration to start straight after payment, you agree that you lose that right once it starts. Even so, you can ask for a refund within 7 days of payment through Hotmart's guarantee: the decoration is then switched off. All prices include VAT. If you're under 18, a parent or carer must make the purchase.

"Make videos like this" waiting list

If you leave your email (and, if you like, your Instagram) in the "Make videos like this" form, we only use it to tell you when the video tool is ready. We don't publish it or share it with anyone. You can ask us to delete it at any time at privacy@ukhousebyhouse.com.

Children

The game is meant for people aged 13 and over, in line with the age of digital consent in the UK. We don't knowingly use the data of anyone under 13: if you think a child under 13 has a house on the map, tell us and we'll remove it. We collect as little data as possible and nothing is shown apart from the username, the town and the points.

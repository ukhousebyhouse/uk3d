Terms and support

UK House by House · an Instagram game with a 3D map · last updated: 2 November 2026

Customer support

Questions about a purchase, a refund or your house? Email privacy@ukhousebyhouse.com or send a direct message on Instagram to @ukhousebyhouse. Please include your Instagram username. We reply within 2 working days.

The game

UK House by House is a free game. If you comment your town and country on a post by @ukhousebyhouse on Instagram, you get a house on the map at ukhousebyhouse.com with your username. The full rules and how we use your data are in our rules and privacy notice. By playing or buying, you agree to these terms.

What you can buy

  • House decorations and rare houses for your own house, or as a gift for another player's house: team colours, golden house, neon and other effects, or a one-of-a-kind house design (lighthouse, castle, windmill and so on).
  • Each purchase costs the price shown before you pay (currently £4.99, VAT included) and stays visible on the map for 10 days from payment. It's a one-off payment, not a subscription: nothing renews by itself.
  • Each rare house can be held by only one house per town at the same time. If someone in the same town already has it, you can't buy it until theirs ends.
  • Decorations are for looks only: they give no points, roles or advantage in the game, and nobody can buy points or roles.
  • You can always try decorations for free on the map before paying. Gifts show "🎁 from @your_account" above the other player's house, so both accounts must already have a house on the map.

Shops and billboards for businesses

  • A local business can put a 3D shop or a billboard on the map of a town that already has houses, with its name, colour, logo, a short description and a WhatsApp button. The price depends on the size of the town and is shown before you pay (VAT included). It's a one-off payment for 30 days from approval: nothing renews by itself.
  • Each town has a limited number of shops and billboards. Your space is held once you've paid; we check every business before it goes live, normally within 24 hours. A shop takes a plot in town and keeps it; a billboard goes in the next free billboard spot in town (the best spots go first).
  • We don't accept betting, piracy, adult content, politics, weapons, prescription drugs, crypto or investments, loans, or anything offensive or misleading. If we don't approve your business, we refund you in full. You can also ask for a full refund within 7 days of payment if it hasn't gone live yet.
  • If you name your player account when you order, that house gets the points and Patron badge shown on the order page: they grow the house but don't count in elections.

Payment

Payments are processed securely by our payment provider (Stripe). We never see or store your card details. You'll get a receipt by email. If you're under 18, a parent or carer must make the purchase.

Cancellation and refunds

For digital content you normally have 14 days to cancel. When you ask for the decoration to start straight after payment, you agree that you lose that right once it starts. Even so, we'll refund you if you ask within 7 days of payment: just email privacy@ukhousebyhouse.com with your Instagram username, and the decoration is switched off. If a decoration doesn't appear on the map within 24 hours of payment, or the map is unavailable for most of its 10 days, we'll fix it or refund you in full. This doesn't affect your statutory rights as a consumer.

Gifts and fair use

Anyone who receives a gift can ask us to remove it at any time (the buyer is then refunded). We may remove a decoration, without refund, if it's used to harass another player, or if the house is removed from the map at its owner's request or for breaking the game rules.

Challenges and prizes

Sometimes we run short challenges, announced on @ukhousebyhouse with a countdown on the map (for example "Bring Your Mates": whoever brings the most new players before the deadline wins a decorated house). Unless the post says otherwise:

  • A new player counts for you if you tagged them in a comment on one of our posts and they get their first house on the map (by commenting their town and country) during the challenge, UK time. Each new player counts once, for the first person who tagged them.
  • The winner is whoever has the most new players when the challenge ends. On a tie, whoever reached that number first wins. Results come from the same map update that everyone can see.
  • Free to enter, no purchase needed. Fake, duplicate or bought accounts don't count, and we may disqualify anyone who tries to game the challenge.
  • The prize is a decoration of the winner's choice from the shop (including a rare house, if it's free in their town) for 10 days. It has no cash value and can't be exchanged. We contact the winner on Instagram; if they don't reply within 7 days, the prize goes to the runner-up.
  • Challenges are run by UK House by House only. They aren't sponsored, endorsed or run by Instagram or Meta.

Availability and changes

We do our best to keep the map online and up to date, but it may sometimes be slow or offline for maintenance. We may update the game, the prices of future purchases and these terms; purchases already made keep the terms that applied when you paid. If we ever close the game, we'll refund the days left on any active decoration.
